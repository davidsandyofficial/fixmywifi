Privacy

  • No accounts, no analytics, no trackers, no cookies.
  • Your tests, rooms, checkup answers and plan progress are saved only in your browser (localStorage). "Clear history" deletes them.
  • Running a speed test downloads and uploads test data to Cloudflare's public speed-test servers (speed.cloudflare.com), which see your IP address like any website. Their policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • Amazon links include an Associates tag. If you click one, Amazon's own privacy policy applies on their site. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
  • Share buttons only open X or your phone's share sheet. Nothing is posted for you.
