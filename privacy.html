Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

  • No accounts, analytics, ads, trackers or cookies. No third-party scripts are loaded.
  • Your speed tests, rooms, checkup answers and plan progress are stored only in your browser (localStorage) on this device. They are never sent to us. "Clear history" in the app, or clearing your browser's site data, deletes them.
  • When you run a speed test, your browser downloads and uploads random test data to Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com). Like any website, Cloudflare receives your IP address, browser information and the test traffic. Cloudflare's policy applies: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits (including IP addresses) for security: GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Amazon links include an Associates tag that tells Amazon the visit came from this site. On Amazon, Amazon's privacy notice and cookies apply. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
  • Share buttons only open X or your device's share sheet with text filled in. Nothing is ever posted for you.
  • Calendar reminders are files that download to your device and contain only a link to this app.
