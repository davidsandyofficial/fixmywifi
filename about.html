About FixMyWiFi

FixMyWiFi is a free WiFi doctor built by @DavidSandy. It measures your connection, maps weak rooms, and builds a step-by-step fix plan, with free fixes first.

Speed tests use Cloudflare's public speed-test endpoints (speed.cloudflare.com). Results are estimates and can differ from other speed tests.

Product suggestions link to Amazon search results so you can compare models and prices yourself. We never show made-up prices, ratings or reviews.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. That helps keep this tool free, and it never changes what the plan recommends.
