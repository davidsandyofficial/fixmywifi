About FixMyWiFi

FixMyWiFi is a free, independent WiFi helper made by @DavidSandy. It measures your connection, maps weak rooms, and builds a step-by-step fix plan, with free fixes first.

Speed test powered by Cloudflare. Tests run against Cloudflare's public speed-test endpoints (speed.cloudflare.com/__down and /__up), the same defaults used by Cloudflare's MIT-licensed @cloudflare/speedtest library. FixMyWiFi doesn't bundle that library, and results can differ from other speed tests.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Product links go to normal Amazon search pages so you can compare models and prices yourself. FixMyWiFi never shows Amazon prices, ratings or reviews, and earning a commission never changes what the plan recommends. Free fixes are always listed first.
